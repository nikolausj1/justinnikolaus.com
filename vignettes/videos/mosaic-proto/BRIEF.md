## Intent

A prototype for the project vignettes on justinnikolaus.com. One project, Mosaic (an iPhone photo collage app), built four ways so Justin can decide two open questions by looking: whether each vignette opens on the product mark (app icon on brand color, then the moment, loop returns to the mark) or carries no mark in the video, and whether the phone is shown as a real device frame on a stage with slight 3D tilt or as bare UI in a rounded rectangle with camera pushes. Slow and deliberate, Apple product page pace: one or two long pushes, at most one cut, a hold. Dramatic, professional, intentional. The clip loops in a 3:2 card at about 600 px wide, muted, no controls.

## Assets

- assets/mosaic-screen-1206.mp4: 1206x2622, 6.6 s, 30 fps. iPhone 17 simulator recording of the Mosaic editor: four photos, two seam drags that reflow the collage. The hero footage.
- assets/mosaic-icon.png: 1024 px app icon, near-black field with six blue rounded squares. The mark.
- assets/mosaic-finished.jpg: a finished collage export from the app, for the closing beat.

## Customizations

- Four renders from two compositions: `device` (framed phone on a stage) and `bare` (UI only), each with the mark intro on and off.
- No text of any kind in the video. Captions live in the site HTML.
- Poster is the first frame. Loop seam must be clean: last frame returns to the first.

## Notes

- Brand: the icon's blues (#0a3cff to #6fb6ff) on near-black (#0b0b0d). The stage for the device variant is dark and tinted toward that blue. The bare variant sits on the same field.
- Photos in the footage are a family at a fun run; they are already public on the site.
- No em dashes anywhere, including file names and comments.
