What to order, and what to say

Four stops: the countertop, the paint, Home Depot for lumber and supplies, and IKEA. The rest ships.

1. Countertop

What to ask for
Species
Hard maple. It's the hardest common option, and its tight grain doesn't trap marker or glitter.
Build
Edge grain, full-length strips. That's the standard countertop style; end grain is the pricey checkerboard look.
Thickness
1 1/2 in. Standard, nothing special to request.
Depth
25 in. Also standard.
Lengths
58 in and 45 in (measure first, below)
Finish
Factory film finish (varnish, conversion varnish or acrylic polyurethane), satin or semi-gloss. Not oil: oil needs re-oiling, and marker soaks in.
Edges
The 45 in piece needs one square, sharp end where it meets the long piece. Light ease on the other visible edges is fine.
Holes
None. You'll drill the two cord holes after install.
Measure before you order.
  • Back piece: measure wall to wall at 29.5 in high, both at the wall and 25 in out. Use the smaller number minus 1/8 in (about 58 in).
  • Right piece: measure from 25 in off the back wall to the outside corner (about 45 in).
Why no holes in the order: where they go depends on where the cleat and the outlet end up. Drilling after install takes a minute with a 2 in hole saw (tape the spot first so the finish doesn't chip, and drill from the top), and a hole drilled in the wrong place can't be undone. Seal the raw edge with a dab of poly, or let the grommet cover it.

Skip these (they look like butcher block but aren't what you want)
  • Home Depot Hampton Bay "Finished Engineered" (about $141 to $203): a 3 mm wood layer over particleboard, oil-finished.
  • IKEA KARLBY: 1/8 in veneer over particleboard; cut ends show particleboard.
  • Live-edge Saman (Home Depot, Lowe's): the wavy edge can't make a tight joint at the corner.
  • Dunn Lumber, Floor & Decor: no prefinished butcher block (Floor & Decor's is all unfinished, 8 and 12 ft).
Say this: calling Crosscut

"Hi, I'm looking for John Boos maple countertop, 1 1/2 inch thick, 25 inches deep, prefinished with Varnique. Do you have the 48 and 60 inch lengths in stock?"

"Can you crosscut them for me: the 60 down to 58 inches, and the 48 down to 45? What do you charge per cut, and is the cut square and clean enough to butt against the other top?"

"What's the price for both, and can I pick up today?"

"Do you sell something to seal the cut ends, like EZ-DO poly gel?"

Say or type this: custom order online

"Two solid hard maple, full-length edge-grain butcher block tops, 1 1/2 inches thick, factory prefinished with a film finish (conversion varnish or acrylic polyurethane, satin), not oil. Piece A: 58 by 25 inches. Piece B: 45 by 25 inches. Finish all six sides. Piece B's ends square and sharp, no ease; light ease on the other top edges. No holes."

"What's your tolerance on length and squareness? Please quote freight to my residential address in Seattle, with and without liftgate, and ship flat on a pallet with corner protection."

2. Paint

Say this at Sherwin-Williams

"One gallon of Emerald Interior Acrylic Latex, satin, in Distance, SW 6243. I'm a PaintPerks member, so please apply the fall sale. Could you shake it and dab a little on the lid so I can check the color? A stir stick and a lid opener too, please."

The Fall PaintPerks Sale runs through Oct 12: 35% off Emerald and 15% off Purdy brushes. PaintPerks is free; sign up online or at the counter first. The store picks the base (likely Deep Base).

How the bench goes together

2x4 cleats screwed to the wall studs carry the back edge, two legs carry the front, and figure-8 fasteners hold the top down while letting the wood move with humidity. The numbers are the order you install things.

  1. Right cleat, 2x4 cut to 68 1/2 in. On edge, top at 28 in, two 3 1/2 in screws into every stud.
  2. Left cleat, 2x4, 23 in, into the back-left corner.
  3. Back cleat, 2x4, about 55 in, between the two side cleats.
  4. Figure-8 fasteners, about every 16 in along the cleat tops, loose lobe toward the room.
  5. Leg under the seam, standing on the mat; its top plate spans the joint.
  6. Leg at the open end, 2 in in from the end and the front edge.
  7. Back top, 58 x 25, set first.
  8. Right top, 45 x 25, butts into the back top's front edge.
  9. Mending plates, 3 or 4, across the seam underneath, 3/4 in screws.

What goes in the space

Storage

  • SKADIS pegboards: everyday tools: scissors, tape, rulers, cups of markers, crayons and pencils.
  • LACK shelves at 56 in: back for projects in progress; right for finished builds, visible from the theater. Rated 22 lb each, so light builds only.
  • ALEX drawers under the left end, 1.5 in off both walls to clear the cleats. Drawers: paper, drawing refills, paint and brushes, glue/tape/string, odds and ends.
  • Upper shelves at 74 and 87 in: clear bins for spare build boxes, LEGO sets and bulk supplies.
  • Back-right corner: tall projects on top, scrap bin for cardboard underneath.

Power and light

  • Surge strip on the right wall under the bench, next to the existing outlet.
  • Two holes in the right bench, centered 4 in from the wall so they miss the cleat: a 2 in cord grommet near the seam for the light cords, and a desk power grommet near the ChompSaw.
  • LED bars under each LACK shelf, set to 4000K. The LACK is hollow, so use 1/2 in screws or mounting tape.

Floor and seating

  • Bamboo chair mat rated for plush carpet, tucked against the back wall. The drawers and corner leg stand on it.
  • Adjustable chairs: at a 29.5 in bench, kids 50 to 56 in tall need a seat about 20 in high with their feet supported 4.5 to 6 in up. The Keekaroo's seat and footplate both adjust, up to adult size.

Whiteboard and ChompSaw

  • Whiteboard: 35 x 23 magnetic, hung vertically on the left wall, bottom at 30 in, centered between the bench and the door casing.
  • ChompSaw: clamp it at the front edge of the right bench near the opening so big cardboard hangs out into the room. About 70 to 75 dB, roughly a vacuum.
