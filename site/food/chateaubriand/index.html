Chateaubriand

With shallot and red wine sauce

Adapted from Stacey Ballis’s Chateaubriand recipe at Delish.

Serves
4–8
Prep
10 min
Chill
1–2 hr
Roast
60–90 min

Ingredients

Beef tenderloin

Shallot and red wine sauce

Directions

  1. Salt and chill. Salt the tenderloin on all sides. Set it on a wire rack in a rimmed sheet pan and refrigerate uncovered for 1–2 hours, or up to 8 hours if prepping ahead.

  2. Start the roast. Heat the oven to 250°F with a rack in the middle. Rub the chilled beef with 2 tablespoons of softened butter and roast on the wire rack. Rotate the pan every 30 minutes if the oven heats unevenly.

  3. Check the center. Start checking after 45 minutes. Insert an instant-read thermometer from one end so the tip reaches the middle. Delish gives 120°F for rare, 125°F for medium-rare and 130°F for medium, with an estimated roasting time of 60–90 minutes. For food safety, the USDA recommends 145°F before removing the beef from the oven and a rest of at least 3 minutes. USDA guidance.

  4. Reduce the sauce. While the beef cooks, warm the oil in a small pan over medium heat. Soften the shallot for about 3 minutes without browning it. Add the wine and simmer until about ¼ cup remains. Stir in the broth and optional brown sugar, then simmer until the liquid is reduced by half.

  5. Whisk in the butter. Take the pan off the heat. Whisk in the cold butter one piece at a time until the sauce is glossy. Stir in the herbs and lemon juice, then season with salt and pepper.

  6. Rest and serve. Rub the roast with the remaining 2 tablespoons of softened butter, loosely tent it with foil and rest for 20–30 minutes. Remove the twine and cut the roast into ½-inch slices. Gently warm the sauce and spoon it over the beef.
