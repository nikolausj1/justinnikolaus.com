About the roast

Dinner timeline

Use these times to plan dinner. Start the rest once the thermometer reaches your target temperature, and allow more time if the beef needs it.

  1. 4 hours before dinner Salt the beef and set it on a wire rack in the refrigerator.
  2. 3 hours 30 minutes before If using compound butter or salsa verde, make it now. Chill the butter until firm. If you roast the salsa vegetables at 450°F, give the oven time to cool back to 250°F.
  3. 2 hours 15 minutes before Set the oven to 250°F for the beef.
  4. 2 hours before Butter the beef and put it in the preheated oven.
  5. 1 hour 30 minutes before If using the shallot and red wine sauce, start the reduction. Keep the cubed butter cold.
  6. 1 hour 15 minutes before Take the first temperature reading 45 minutes after the roast goes in.
  7. 30 minutes before Aim to have the beef out of the oven and resting for 20–30 minutes. Finish the warm sauce or get the compound butter or salsa ready.
  8. At dinner Remove the twine, slice the beef and serve with your chosen sauce.

Shopping and equipment

For Thanksgiving 2026, we got the beef from Met Market.

For four main-course servings, plan on about 1½ lb of trimmed beef. For eight, buy two roasts of about 1½ lb each and double the salt and butter for the beef. Check the temperature of each roast separately.

Ask the butcher for trimmed, center-cut beef tenderloin tied at 2-inch intervals. Check the ingredient list for your chosen sauce before shopping.

For the roast, set out a rimmed sheet pan, wire rack, instant-read thermometer, foil, cutting board and sharp knife.

For the sauces, use a small saucepan and whisk for the shallot sauce, a small saucepan, mixing bowl and plastic wrap for the compound butter, or a sheet pan and blender for the salsa.

Roast ingredients

Roast directions

  1. Salt and chill. Salt the tenderloin on all sides. Set it on a wire rack in a rimmed sheet pan and refrigerate uncovered for 1–2 hours, or up to 8 hours if prepping ahead.

  2. Start the roast. Heat the oven to 250°F with a rack in the middle. Rub the chilled beef with 2 tablespoons of softened butter and roast on the wire rack. Rotate the pan every 30 minutes if the oven heats unevenly.

  3. Check the center. Start checking after 45 minutes. Insert an instant-read thermometer from one end so the tip reaches the middle. Delish gives 120°F for rare, 125°F for medium-rare and 130°F for medium, with an estimated roasting time of 60–90 minutes.

  4. Rest and serve. Rub the roast with the remaining 2 tablespoons of softened butter, loosely tent it with foil and rest for 20–30 minutes. Remove the twine, cut the roast into ½-inch slices and serve with your chosen sauce.

The 250°F oven and doneness temperatures are Delish’s. USDA home-cooking guidance calls for an oven at 325°F or higher, beef at 145°F or higher before it comes out of the oven, and a rest of at least 3 minutes. USDA oven guidance · USDA beef guidance.

Shallot and red wine sauce

Ingredients

Directions

  1. Warm the oil over medium heat. Soften the shallot for about 3 minutes without letting it brown. Add the wine and simmer down to about ¼ cup.

  2. Add the broth and optional brown sugar, then simmer until the liquid is reduced by half.

  3. Take the pan off the heat. Whisk in the cold butter one piece at a time until glossy. Stir in the herbs and lemon juice, then season with salt and pepper.

  4. Warm gently if needed and spoon over the sliced beef.

Red wine compound butter

Makes about 1 cup. Adapted from Lena Abraham’s red wine butter at Delish.

Ingredients

Directions

  1. Bring the wine, rosemary, bay leaf and honey to a boil. Lower to a simmer and reduce until syrupy, about 2 tablespoons after 10–12 minutes. Discard the herbs and let the reduction cool.

  2. Beat the softened butter with the garlic, cooled reduction, salt and pepper. Stir in the chives.

  3. Shape into a log in plastic wrap and refrigerate until firm. Slice into pats and set them on the warm beef.

Roasted tomatillo salsa verde

Makes about 2 cups. Adapted from Kat Boytsova’s salsa verde at Delish.

Ingredients

Directions

  1. Heat the oven to 450°F. Toss the tomatillos, onion and jalapeños with the vegetable oil, salt and pepper on a sheet pan.

  2. Roast for about 20 minutes, until the vegetables are soft and blistered in spots. Let them cool before blending.

  3. Blend the vegetables with the cilantro and lime juice. Drizzle in the olive oil while blending until the salsa is mostly smooth. Adjust the salt and pepper and serve with the beef.
